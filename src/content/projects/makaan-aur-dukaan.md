## The brief

Makaan Aur Dukaan has been in Mumbai real estate since 2004. It covers everything from ultra-luxury residences and commercial leasing to pre-leased investments, land deals and capital advisory. The firm needed a site that felt as discreet and premium as the properties it handles, while making a wide range of services easy to understand.

## What we built

- **An editorial layout** with numbered sections and generous white space. It reads like a luxury brochure, not a listings portal.
- **Six clear practice areas**, each with its own page, so buyers, tenants, investors and landowners each find their path quickly.
- **A selected portfolio** of property cards with type and status labels.
- **Trust signals** throughout: firm heritage, founder's message, client brands and testimonials.
- **Easy contact** by WhatsApp and email, plus a dedicated page for owners to list a property.
