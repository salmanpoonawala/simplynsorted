## The brief

Rayasha Khan is a women's fashion label creating thoughtfully designed Indian occasionwear, with expressive silhouettes and timeless craft. The label needed a store that felt as refined as the clothes and made buying a festive or formal outfit simple.

## What we built

- **A minimal, elegant design** in soft cream tones that lets the collections lead.
- **Shop by category:** kurta sets, lehengas, sarees, gowns and fusion pieces.
- **Dedicated sections** for the full Collection, Ready to Ship pieces, MAKHSUS, and The Label's story.
- **Cart with a Buy Now option** that continues the purchase on WhatsApp, so customers can talk to the brand directly about sizing and styling.
