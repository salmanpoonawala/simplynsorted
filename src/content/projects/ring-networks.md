## The brief

Ring Networks has provided internet services since the early 2000s, to homes, offices, institutions and live events. It needed a site that turned technical services into clear offers for each type of customer, and captured enquiries from anywhere on the page.

## What we built

- **Calls to action in the hero** for the two biggest offers: leased lines and business broadband.
- **Use-case sections** for streaming, gaming, meetings, corporate events, work from home and online classes.
- **Credibility built in:** live counters, client and partner logos, and testimonials.
- **City pages** for Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Hyderabad and Bangalore, plus a blog and FAQ for search visibility.
- **Enquiry everywhere:** an "Enquire Now" form, a chat widget and a click-to-call number.
