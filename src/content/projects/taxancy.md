## The brief

Taxancy Financial Advisory has helped businesses and individuals with tax, compliance and registrations since 2013. Its services list is long, from company and GST registration to returns, payroll and corporate advisory. The site had to make that list easy to scan and give visitors confidence to get in touch.

## What we built

- **Services grouped into four simple categories:** Registration, Returns, Payroll and Corporate Advisory.
- **Clear reasons to trust the firm** up front: expertise, confidentiality, timely service and a personal approach.
- **An About page** covering the firm's story, values and team.
- **A contact page** with a form, WhatsApp, phone, email and business hours.
