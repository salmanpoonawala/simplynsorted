## The brief

Zah Beauty sells skincare and haircare for everyday concerns like dark spots, dryness and frizz. It has stores in Mumbai, Mumbai Airport, Goa and Dubai, and also sells on Amazon and Flipkart. It needed its own store that felt as cheerful as the brand and made buying direct the easiest option.

## What we built

- **Shop by concern:** Skin, Hair and Combos, so customers land on the right product fast.
- **A complete store** with cart, discount codes, product reviews and customer accounts, selling in both INR and the UAE.
- **Corporate gifting and bulk order pages** to bring in larger B2B orders.
- **Store locator** for every physical location, and a link across to the sister brand, Zah Salon & Spa.
- **A playful brand voice** carried through banners, navigation and product cards.
