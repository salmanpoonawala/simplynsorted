## The brief

ZAH Salon & Spa offers everything from body spa therapy and Korean head spa to nails, lashes and hair care, across four cities. The website needed to feel relaxing from the first scroll, explain a broad menu of services simply, and help visitors find their nearest salon.

## What we built

- **A calm visual style** with soft, earthy tones, a muted teal, and plenty of breathing room.
- **A services grid** covering six treatment categories, each with its own detail page.
- **Locations for every city** with addresses, map links and phone numbers.
- **Brand story and values**, including the cruelty-free commitment and the founder's mission.
- **WhatsApp chat, FAQ, blog and franchise enquiries**, so every kind of visitor has a next step.
